## Base-case headlines (per 100 MW solar block, 25 years, 10% discount rate, ex-GST)
- NPV of GridMats roads vs bitumen + WBM: about ₹4.1 Cr (conservative ₹3.3 Cr, optimistic ₹7.7 Cr; roads-only ₹2.3 Cr)
- Up-front premium ₹3.5 Cr (0.9% of plant capex); IRR 21%; payback 4.7 years
- 25-year cost per m²: S1 30% below bitumen, 33% below concrete; S2 6% below WBM
- GridMats Solar Walkway: payback about 5.6 years (Navi Mumbai) and 8.1 years (Jamnagar)
- Pilot: Reliance pays about ₹55.3 L for ₹68.5 L of scope; grid at ₹900/m², 23% below the 2025 trial price

Assumptions marked "Assumption" on the Inputs sheet (soiling share, walkway yield and life, upkeep rates) are estimates; the pilot is designed to measure the uncertain ones.
